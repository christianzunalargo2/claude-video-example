Ecuador · Andes · 1°28′ S

CHIMBORAZO

Why is it the country’s highest mountain?

The record · 6,263 m

Taller than every other peak in Ecuador

366 m above Cotopaxi, its closest rival.

Reason 1 · Plate tectonics

Nazca plate dives under South America

Rising magma builds volcanoes on top of the already-high Andes.

Reason 2 · Built in layers

Three volcanoes stacked into one

~120,000 years of eruptions piled lava and ash ever higher.

Bonus · Earth’s bulge

The farthest point from Earth’s center

The equator bulges, lifting its summit past Everest’s.
